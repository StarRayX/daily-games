What is par?

Par is the fewest guards a perfect solver needs for today's case. It reads the same arrows you saw and, every turn, posts the guard that leaves him the fewest hiding nests.

Match par and you played as well as the solver. Beat it and you got a lucky read. There are no community numbers yet, so this is the only comparison.
